# Recorded effort

The visual language starts with repetition: a set recorded, another session completed, one more point added to a chart. A fixed grid and recurring phone proportions make those small actions feel connected. Meticulous alignment gives each page the same calm rhythm.

Charcoal carries the interface. Lime marks the useful moments: a primary action, a completed set, a trace of progress. The accent stays deliberate, leaving the actual product screens to provide the detail. The feature graphic reverses that balance with a bright field that remains distinct in a store listing.

Large, direct typography introduces one idea at a time. Short supporting lines explain the feature without competing with it. The screenshot is the evidence, preserved as rendered by the app; the frame gives it a place rather than changing its contents. Careful spacing and crisp letterforms matter more than added decoration.

Across the set, the same margins, screen scale and fine border treatment form a recognizable sequence. Each image should work alone at a small size and also feel considered beside the others. The final pass focuses on legibility, truthful feature descriptions and precise edges.
